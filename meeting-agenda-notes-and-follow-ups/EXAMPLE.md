# Meeting agenda, notes and follow-ups: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Brackenfold Mechanical is an invented 40-person heating and cooling contractor. The people, figures and jobs are invented too. Priya, the operations manager, runs the weekly operations meeting. She uses the capability twice: on Friday 30 October 2026 with her topics, and on Tuesday 3 November straight after the meeting with her notes.

Settings used: reading goes out one working day before the meeting; the closing read-back takes 5 minutes; the follow-up goes to everyone invited.

## Before the meeting

### What Priya supplied

```
Today is Fri 30 Oct 2026.
Weekly operations meeting Tue 3 Nov 2026, 9:00, 45 min.
Me (Priya, ops manager), Marcus (service manager), Elena (install manager),
Tom (warehouse lead), Jo (office manager).

Topics:
- Winter on-call rota - need to pick between 1-week and 2-week rotations. Marcus. 15 min
- Van 7 - repair or replace? Quote is $6,200 to repair. Tom. 10 min
- Install backlog update. Elena. 10 min
- New scheduling software
- Parts returns - Tom wants to talk about it. 10 min

Open from last time (27 Oct):
- Jo: send revised call-out price sheet to dispatch - due 30 Oct
- Elena: confirm crane date for the Larch Street job - due 2 Nov
- Marcus: order two CO monitors - due 6 Nov
```

### What came back

```
DRAFT AGENDA: Weekly operations meeting
Tuesday 3 November 2026, 9:00 to 9:45 (45 minutes). Run by: Priya (operations manager).
Invited: Marcus (service manager), Elena (install manager), Tom (warehouse lead), Jo (office manager).

To settle before this goes out: 3
1. The topics do not fit. Four timed topics take 45 minutes and the read-back takes 5: 50 minutes
   in a 45-minute meeting, 5 over. Proposal: the install backlog update only informs, so Elena
   sends it in writing and it comes off the agenda. The agenda below shows that proposal applied.
   Say if you would rather change something else.
2. "Parts returns" has no outcome. What should the meeting settle: a new process, who owns
   returns, or something else?
3. "New scheduling software" has no outcome, no lead and no time. It is left off. Add them and it
   goes on.

Read before the meeting, sent by Monday 2 November:
- Install backlog update, in writing (Elena)

1. 9:00 to 9:15 (15 min). Winter on-call rota. Decide: one-week or two-week rotations. Lead: Marcus.
2. 9:15 to 9:25 (10 min). Van 7. Decide: repair at $6,200 or replace. Lead: Tom.
3. 9:25 to 9:35 (10 min). Parts returns. Discuss: [outcome needed]. Lead: Tom.
   9:35 to 9:40 (5 min). Spare.
4. 9:40 to 9:45 (5 min). Read-back. Each decision and action said back to the room with who owns
   it and when it is due, then the open actions below. Lead: Priya.

Open from last time (27 October):
- Send revised call-out price sheet to dispatch. Owner: Jo. Due 30 October.
- Confirm crane date for the Larch Street job. Owner: Elena. Due 2 November.
- Order two CO monitors. Owner: Marcus. Due 6 November.

Note-taking key: D decision. A action, owner and due date. P parked. Q question. F fact.

Draft for Priya. Nothing has been sent and no invitation has been changed.
```

Priya answered the second question (“agree who owns returns and what the process is”), left the scheduling software for another week and sent the agenda herself.

## After the meeting

### What Priya supplied

The agenda she sent (the one above, with her answer on parts returns) and these notes:

```
Today is Tue 3 Nov 2026. My notes:
ops mtg 3 nov - all there except Jo (out sick), started 9:05
rota - went with 2 week rotations, starts 16 Nov. Marcus to publish the rota by Fri. Elena not
happy about install techs being on it - agreed install techs only cover weekends, revisit after
January
van 7 - Tom says quote went up to 6,800 after they opened it up. not deciding today, need a
replacement price to compare. someone needs to get a lease quote
parts returns - $4k of unreturned parts sitting in the warehouse, some past the supplier's 30 day
window. Tom + Marcus to sort out a process. need to know what the supplier will still take back -
Tom to call them this week
price sheet - Jo not here, don't know if sent
crane - Elena confirmed, 12 Nov
CO monitors - Marcus ordered, arriving next week
Elena: backlog is 23 jobs, 3 waiting on permits
do we need a second dispatcher for winter? nobody had an answer
```

Priya settled the five gaps with Tom, Marcus and Jo that morning, gave the answers to the assistant, read the updated record and sent the follow-up from her own mailbox.
