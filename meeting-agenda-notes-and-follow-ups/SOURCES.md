# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two open-source packages informed the method: alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT) for the agenda rules, namely an outcome for every topic, decisions first, times that must fit and a closing read-back of decisions and actions, and for the rule that an action with no owner or no date is flagged, never dropped; panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0) for the before-and-after shape of the work, the coded note-taking key and a record that separates decisions, actions, parked items and open questions. Neither package's scripts, meeting-cost pricing, memory file or calendar connection is used. The paste-in form, the gap list, the held-back rule and the unsent follow-up draft are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
