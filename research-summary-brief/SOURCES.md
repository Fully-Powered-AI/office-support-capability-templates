# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One open-source package informed the method: alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT), its research-summarizer. From its main instructions file: working only from documents a person hands over, never searching for more; summarising each source before comparing them; weighing each source's credibility, strength of evidence, age and possible bias; setting out where sources agree and disagree and what none of them covers; noting a missing date or author rather than supplying one; and tying every finding to a place in its source. From its summary-templates reference: warning signs in a report, such as a document written to sell a product, no account of how the data was gathered, and claims larger than the evidence shown. Its scripts, citation styles, scoring grid and recommendation section are not used. The basis label on every finding, the rule that agreement resting on the same data counts once, the check of a document's headline against its own figures, the list of documents cited but not supplied, and leaving the business judgment to the reader are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
