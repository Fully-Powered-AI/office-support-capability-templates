# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One open-source package informed the method: the domestic-trip-planning package in AtlasOmnia/hermes-custom-pack (MIT). Its main instructions gave the ideas of settling the dates and the number of nights before looking at any price and redoing every figure when they move, checking the airport the trip starts from and the town of each place first, setting aside options that break the traveller's needs before comparing the rest, showing where each price was found, and recording why a dearer choice was needed when policy is in question. Its note on flight prices gave pricing the exact route and dates instead of a headline fare, giving the cheapest choice that works and a second one, and leaving an extra charge marked as not priced rather than guessing it. Its family and ski-holiday guidance, its workarounds for pages that will not load, its hotel-site extraction and its historical fare data are not used. The business-trip form of the work, the timing check against fixed meetings with set margins, the check against a pasted travel policy and its approver, the price age that triggers a recheck, the list of bookings still to make with their deadlines, the default option headers with a stop on any mismatch, and the acceptance tests are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
