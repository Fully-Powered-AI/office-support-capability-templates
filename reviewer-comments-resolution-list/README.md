# Reviewer comments into one resolution list

The owner of a document that came back from several reviewers gets one checklist: every proposed edit and comment, grouped by section, with clashes between reviewers set side by side. Every item stays open until the owner decides it, and the document itself is never touched.

*Workflow template · Executive and office support · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/reviewer-comments-resolution-list/)*

## The outcome

Three marked-up copies, two emails and a note from a phone call become one list you can work down in a sitting. Every suggestion is there once, under the name of the person who made it, in their own words, filed under the section of the document it belongs to. Where two reviewers want different things from the same sentence, you see both together and choose.

Nothing slips through. A comment on a table that no longer exists, or a change buried in a chart, is listed for you to check by hand instead of quietly disappearing. Changes to figures, dates, names and promises are marked so they get a second look.

The workflow template never edits, merges or tidies your document, and it never decides who is right. Every item starts open. You decide each one, make the edits in your own document, and reply to your reviewers yourself.

## Who starts the work, and when

The person who owns the document starts it, as soon as the last reviewer's comments are in: the author of a report, the manager pulling together a proposal or plan, the coordinator of a funding application, or the assistant who keeps a board paper moving. It runs again after the owner has made decisions, to record them and show what is still open.

## What they supply

- **The version you are revising,** with its name and section headings.
- **Each reviewer's returned copy,** as a marked-up file or an export of its comments and changes. The template sets the export columns it expects and stops to ask if yours differ.
- **Anything that came another way:** emails, chat messages and call notes, with who sent them and when.
- **Your settings:** your deadline, the kinds of change you want marked for a second look, and whether reviewers appear by name or by role.

Everything is supplied by you. No mailbox or document system is connected.

## What comes out

1. **A header** naming every reviewer, what they sent and how many items it held, with counts that add up.
2. **What to settle first:** each clash, each comment that could not be placed, and each change that needs checking by hand.
3. **The clashes,** each reviewer's version shown one under the other with the reason they cannot both be done.
4. **The checklist by section,** in the order of your document, every item marked Open with room for your decision and note.
5. **The lists at the end:** items not placed, items to check by hand, items marked for a second look, and suggestions two reviewers share.

In a good list, every item traces back to a reviewer's copy, and nobody's comment is missing or reworded.

## What has to change for your company

Set your watch list: the kinds of change that always need a second look in your documents, such as prices, client names or headline figures. Decide whether reviewers appear by name or by role when the list is shared. If your document tool exports comments under other column names, change the defaults once in the template. Agree who signs off a change to a commitment, a price or a statement to a regulator. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini; check whether your product reads tracked changes directly or needs an export, and decide which documents it may be given.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
