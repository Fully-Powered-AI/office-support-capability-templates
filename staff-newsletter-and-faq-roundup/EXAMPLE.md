# Staff newsletter and FAQ roundup: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Brindlecote Packaging is an invented 110-person maker of food packaging. Its people, customers, documents and dates are invented too. Dana, the office manager, puts together the monthly staff newsletter, and Martin, the managing director, approves each issue. On Thursday 26 November 2026 Dana gives the assistant everything below, in full, and gets back the issue check and the draft that follow.

How the inputs became the output, for checking:

- **Rows 1 to 3, 4, 5, 6 and 11** are in the draft: three wins dated in November, three coming-up items dated between 30 November 2026 and 11 January 2027, each headed by the date in its row with the dates in its own words left as submitted, and one welcome with consent "yes". Seven news items against a limit of 15.
- **Row 7** is about one person's health and **row 10** praises one person's results, so both are held out whatever anyone agreed. Neither is described in the issue check.
- **Row 8** is people news with the consent column blank. **Row 9** announces a new way of submitting expenses with no link to an announcement. **Row 12** repeats the October issue's first win.
- **Row 13** has a type the template does not know, so it waits for Dana.
- **Questions 1 to 3** were asked 11, 8 and 6 times, above the threshold of 3, and each is answered by one sentence in one source, quoted word for word; the neighbouring sentences on the carry-over deadline and the visitor spaces answer questions nobody asked, so they are left out. The site safety rules were approved on 10 June 2025, earlier than 30 November 2025, twelve months before the send date, so Tomas is asked to confirm them.
- **Question 4** has no source that answers it. **Question 5** is one person's own pay question. **Question 6** was asked twice.

Dana asks Tomas about the footwear rule and settles row 13 herself, gives the answers to the assistant for an updated draft, and passes it to Martin. Nothing goes to staff until he approves it and Dana sends it.
