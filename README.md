# Fully Powered Capability Templates: Executive and Office Support

Meetings that end in decisions, and decisions that get done. These capability templates take the preparation and follow-through around the working day and give an AI assistant a precise way to handle it, so nothing agreed in a room is lost on the way out.

This repository holds 5 capability templates for executive assistants, office managers and anyone who runs a meeting. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The capabilities

- **[Board meeting pack](board-meeting-pack/)**: Before each board meeting, the CEO gets a pre-read built on the one decision the board is asked to make, with every figure from the company's own reports, an agenda kept mostly for discussion and the questions directors are likely to ask. Afterwards, a record of what was decided. The CEO sends it all.
- **[Decision log](decision-log/)**: Your chief of staff keeps one register of what the leadership actually approved, across every meeting and message: each decision with who approved it, each action with one owner and a date, what is overdue, and a flag wherever a new decision clashes with an earlier one or revives a rejected idea.
- **[Meeting agenda, notes and follow-ups](meeting-agenda-notes-and-follow-ups/)**: Whoever runs the meeting walks in with an agenda where every topic has an outcome and a time, and walks out with a one-page record: what was decided, who owns each action and by when, and what is still open. Nothing reaches anyone until that person has read it and sends it.
- **[Research summary brief](research-summary-brief/)**: An executive handed a pile of reports, articles or papers gets one brief: what each source found, where the sources agree and disagree, the limits of each, and exact quotations cited to the page. Built only from the documents supplied. What it means for the business stays the reader's call.
- **[Reviewer comments into one resolution list](reviewer-comments-resolution-list/)**: The owner of a document that came back from several reviewers gets one checklist: every proposed edit and comment, grouped by section, with clashes between reviewers set side by side. Every item stays open until the owner decides it, and the document itself is never touched.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
