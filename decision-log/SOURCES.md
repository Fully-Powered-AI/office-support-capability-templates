One open-source package informed the method: the decision-logger package in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT), for keeping approved decisions apart from the discussion behind them, the fields of a decision entry with its rejected options and review date, one decision per entry and one owner per action, an earlier entry kept and marked as replaced rather than removed, the checks for a decision that contradicts another, revives a rejected proposal or gives work to a second owner, settling a conflict by replacing the earlier decision or returning it to the approver, reopening a rejected proposal only on the approver's explicit confirmation, and the lists of overdue actions and actions coming due. Its board-meeting scripts, transcript store, automatic loading and tracking program are not used. Approval by a named approver within their limits as the only way into the register, the list of what was not entered, the held-back rule, the choices under the duplicate-action and approver flags, the rule that no owner or date is ever supplied, the export headers with a stop on any mismatch, and the acceptance tests are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
