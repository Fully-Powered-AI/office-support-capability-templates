Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the delegation method in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes: handing each task to a single named person; naming the thing to be handed back instead of the effort to be spent; a firm date for it; telling the person what the work is for and who will read it, its form and size, what is left out of it and what earlier work to build on; asking the person to confirm within a short, fixed time that they have taken it on; spacing check-ins according to how long the task runs; following up more firmly the later a task gets, up to the manager deciding whether to take it back or move it to someone else; explaining any in-house name the person may not know; and treating a task as finished only once the manager has the work and is satisfied with it. From the same package's test cases it takes a weekly view that keeps tasks nobody has confirmed apart from tasks under way, with a next step for every open task. Fully Powered AI added: the line on what the person may decide, spend and contact without asking, with everything else coming back to the manager; what done means; the due time as well as the date; the questions raised for every gap instead of filling it; the working-day rules for the acknowledgement, the midpoint check-in and the final check; the order in which open tasks are grouped and the working-day count of lateness; the escalation point after which no note is drafted and the manager chooses among five courses; requests for more time going to the manager as questions; the default list columns with a stop on any mismatch; the plain, neutral wording of every note; the list changes held until the manager answers; and the rule that remarks about a person are left out and nobody is ever rated, ranked or compared. The source's local configuration file, its log inside that file and its tailoring of messages to each person's style are not used. No third-party text is reused.
