# Delegation brief and follow-up: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Tarnwick Advisory is an invented 30-person consulting firm. Its people, suppliers, tasks and dates are invented too. Marta, the managing director, hands work to people across the firm; Theo, her executive assistant, keeps her delegation list. The example has two parts: a brief written on Monday 9 November 2026, and the weekly list run on Friday 13 November 2026. Each part gives the inputs in full, then what came back.

## Part 1: a brief

### The inputs

#### 1. Settings

```
Today: Monday 9 November 2026
Manager: Marta (managing director)
List keeper: Theo (executive assistant)
Working days: Monday to Friday. Office closed: no days in November or December
Acknowledgement window: default (end of the first working day after handover)
Check-in rule: default
Last delegation number used: D-013
```

#### 2. Marta's note

```
For Jonas. I need a summary of what clients told us in the autumn feedback
survey, for the partners' planning day on Tuesday 1 December. The partners
will pick the two service changes we commit to for next year from it, and I
want to read it before the planning-day pack goes out. Two pages at most, in
the PLX layout: the main themes, how many clients raised each one, and three
or four short quotes. I need it by Friday the 27th at noon. He can ring the
account leads to check what a comment meant, but he shouldn't contact any
clients. No recommendations; choosing the fixes is for the partners. Start
from the survey export in the shared drive (23 responses) and last year's
summary. At the midpoint I'd like to see an outline of the themes. Jonas can
be slow to get going, so keep a close eye on him.
```

### What came back

```
DELEGATION BRIEF: D-014. Autumn client feedback summary
From: Marta. To: Jonas. Handed over: Monday 9 November 2026.
Due: Friday 27 November 2026, 12:00 ("Friday the 27th at noon").

What: a summary of what clients told the firm in the autumn feedback survey.
Format: a document of two pages at most, in the PLX layout (see question 1).
Done means: the main themes, how many clients raised each one, and three or
four short quotes.
Why it matters: the partners will pick from it the two service changes the
firm commits to for next year.
Who will use it: Marta, who reads it before the planning-day pack goes out,
and the partners, at the planning day on Tuesday 1 December 2026.
What you may do without asking: phone the account leads to check what a
comment meant. Anything else: ask Marta first.
Not part of this task: contacting any clients; recommendations, because
choosing the fixes is for the partners.
Start from: the survey export in the shared drive (23 responses) and last
year's summary.

Check-in points
- Acknowledge by the end of Tuesday 10 November 2026: confirm you have this
  and the date works, or say what would make it work.
- Midpoint check-in, Wednesday 18 November 2026: an outline of the themes.
- Final check, Thursday 26 November 2026: where it stands and anything in
  the way.
- Due Friday 27 November 2026, 12:00.

One remark about Jonas was left out; the brief describes the work only.

Questions for Marta before this is handed over: 1
1. Format: the note asks for "the PLX layout". Should the brief explain it,
   or is it already familiar to Jonas?

List line, once Marta approves:
D-014,Summarise the autumn client feedback survey for the partners' planning day,Jonas,2026-11-09,2026-11-27 12:00,,2026-11-18; 2026-11-26,Awaiting acknowledgement,,,

Draft for Marta. Nothing has been sent and nothing has been added to the
delegation list.
```

How the dates were worked out: the task runs 14 working days, from Tuesday 10 to Friday 27 November. That is more than 5, so there is a midpoint check-in on the 7th working day after the handover, Wednesday 18 November; and more than 2, so there is a final check on the working day before the due date, Thursday 26 November.

Marta answered that Jonas uses the PLX layout for every client report. The updated draft read "None. Ready for Marta to hand over." Marta sent the brief to Jonas herself, and Theo added the list line.

How the figures were worked out:

- **Working days late,** counted after the due date up to and including today. D-010: Friday 6, Monday 9, Tuesday 10, Wednesday 11, Thursday 12 and Friday 13 November, 6 days, which reaches the escalation point of 5, so no note was drafted. D-011: Thursday 12 and Friday 13 November, 2 days, but Priya asked for a new date, so the next step is Marta's answer.
- **Acknowledgement:** D-015 was handed over on Tuesday 10 November, so confirmation was due by the end of Wednesday 11 November; none had come. Jonas confirmed D-014 on Tuesday 10 November, inside its window, so it moves to In progress.
- **Due soon:** today to the fifth working day after today, Friday 13 to Friday 20 November. D-015 is also due on Friday 20 November but sits under "Not yet acknowledged", which comes first.
- **Check-in notes:** the next check-in dates inside the same window are Tuesday 17 November for D-013 (its final check) and Wednesday 18 November for D-014 (its midpoint, set to show the outline of the themes). D-016's next check-in is Tuesday 24 November, outside the window.

Theo took the three questions to Marta that afternoon and made the changes she approved in the list himself. Marta approved the three notes, sent the one to Ade that afternoon, and kept the other two for the dates shown.
