# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Three open-source packages informed the method: anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) for ordering what needs the owner by what a day's delay would cost, pulling out the ask, the amounts, who is waiting and any earlier promise, asking what the owner promised when only pasted mail is supplied, holding bank-detail, payment and log-in requests with no draft and the sender's address checked letter by letter, leaving alone a thread a colleague has already answered, telling a bill from a receipt, drafts that commit to no price or date the owner has not given, and reporting mail that needs no reply as a single count broken down by kind rather than item by item; alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT) for the drafts-only rule, routing unusual or money matters straight to the owner, undrafted, and keeping a list of follow-ups with their status, flagging those due today or overdue even when nothing new has arrived; GTMify/aigtm (MIT) for the split between replies due today and replies that can wait, handing work to the right colleague with a short forwarding note, and drafts that answer the core ask first and buy time honestly when the answer is not known. None of the packages' mailbox connections, scripts, knowledge-base files, colour coding or automatic filing is used. The export format and its header check, the reply-by date, the private group and the question list are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
