# Presentation outline and talk track: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Pelterwick Facilities is an invented office cleaning and maintenance firm with one depot. Its people, sites, figures and prospects are invented too. Maren, the managing director, has a three-minute slot at the quarterly board meeting. She uses the workflow template twice: on Monday 9 November 2026 with her notes, and on Monday 16 November with the slides she has built.

Settings used: the default pace values (steady 120, ordinary 140, quick 160 words a minute), 10 per cent of the slot left unscripted, a slide limit of one slide for every 30 seconds of the slot, and the default shape.

## First pass: the storyboard

### What Maren supplied

```
Today is Mon 9 Nov 2026.
Me: Maren, managing director, Pelterwick Facilities.
Quarterly board meeting, Thu 19 Nov 2026. I have a 3-minute slot.
Questions come after, outside the slot. The chair hands to me and I hand back.
Board: the chair (founder and owner), two non-executive directors, Callum
(finance director). They know the business well. They've heard me complain
about travel but have never seen the numbers.
What I want: their go-ahead to bring a full costed plan for a second depot
in the west to the March board. Not a decision on the depot itself.
Working title: Our distance problem.
Shape: whatever works. Pace: not sure.

My notes:
- one depot, east side of the city
- 41 client sites (a new one started in October). 9 are more than 50 min
  drive from the depot, all of them in the west - these 9 are what I call
  the far sites
- Q3 2026: travel = 22% of crew hours at the far sites, 8% at the rest
- Q3 2026 average call-out response: far sites 3h10, others 1h25
- lost 3 clients last year, 2 were far sites, both said slow call-outs
- 2 prospects in the west said no this year because of distance. one in writing
- staff turnover down to 14% this year
- the plan would cover site, cost, staffing, timing
```

"More than twice as long" is worked out from the notes: 3h10 is 190 minutes and 1h25 is 85 minutes, and 190 divided by 85 is 2.2.

Maren answered: "Approved, headlines as drafted. The second prospect told Rafe on the phone; fine to say. I'll get the weekly driving hours from Callum." She built the five slides herself and wrote her speaker notes.
