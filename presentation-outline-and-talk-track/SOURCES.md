# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two open-source packages informed the method. manojbajaj95/claude-gtm-plugin (MIT), through its slide-outline package: settling the occasion, the audience and what they should do before anything is written; building the talk on one message that says what it means for this audience, its supporting points and the facts behind them; headlines that state each slide's point and, read in order, carry the story; one point to a slide; leading with the conclusion; and agreeing the storyboard before anything further is written. microsoft/cat-agent-skills (MIT), through its presentation talk track builder: a first-person spoken script for each slide with a bridge into it, delivery cues such as pauses, and a handover at the end; the presenter's speaker notes deciding wherever they disagree with the slide, and a note after the script of every place it differs from the slide; sizing the script from the slot and a speaking pace, with a timing table and lines to drop if the talk runs long; marking a gap for the presenter instead of filling it; flagging material marked as not yet public; and reading slides supplied as pictures with no notes behind them. Neither package's slide copywriting guidance, file scripts or deck editing is used. The unscripted allowance, the slide limit, the default three-part shape, the list of every fact used with where it came from, and the limits on what a cut may remove are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
