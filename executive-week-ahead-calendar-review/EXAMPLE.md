# Executive week-ahead calendar review: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Company A is an invented property management company with about 60 staff. Every person, client, supplier, place and meeting below is invented. Sam is the executive assistant to Morgan, the managing director. On Thursday 12 November 2026 Sam exported Morgan's calendar for the week of 16 November, typed up the two priorities Morgan named that morning, and ran the review. First come the inputs Sam supplied, in full, then the brief that came back.

## The inputs

```
Executive: Morgan (managing director). Assistant: Sam.
Week: Monday 16 to Friday 20 November 2026. Times are local, one time zone.
Working days Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 18:00. Base: Office.
Protected: Wednesday 08:30 to 09:30 (school run).
Everything else: the defaults.
```

## How the figures were reached

Not part of the brief: the arithmetic behind it, so a reader can check every figure against the inputs.

- **Monday free time:** working hours 08:30 to 18:00 are 9 h 30 min. Meetings cover 09:00 to 12:30 (3 h 30 min) and 15:00 to 16:30 (1 h 30 min, the overlap counted once): 5 h. 9 h 30 min less 5 h is 4 h 30 min.
- **Tuesday:** 30 + 60 + 45 + 60 + 60 minutes of meetings is 4 h 15 min; the declined lunch is left out. 9 h 30 min less 4 h 15 min is 5 h 15 min.
- **Wednesday:** meetings 60 + 60 + 45 minutes is 2 h 45 min. Busy time is 08:30 to 10:00 (the school run and the committee together, 1 h 30 min), the workshop (1 h), the owner call (45 min) and the private event (1 h): 4 h 15 min. 9 h 30 min less 4 h 15 min is 5 h 15 min.
- **Thursday:** meetings 60 + 120 + 120 minutes is 5 h, the dinner included. Inside working hours the budget session, the preparation block and the board meeting cover 4 h. 9 h 30 min less 4 h is 5 h 30 min.
- **Friday:** 60 + 30 + 60 minutes is 2 h 30 min. 9 h 30 min less 2 h 30 min is 7 h.
- **R2:** on Tuesday the only gap before the 10:00 review is 08:30 to 08:50, 20 minutes; the 09:30 to 10:00 gap is the journey to Client B offices. On Monday the latest gap is 16:40 to 18:00, so the 30 minutes end at 18:00.
- **R4:** on Thursday the gaps before 14:00 are 09:40 to 12:20 and 13:40 to 13:50. The latest with 30 minutes of room is the first, so R4 ends at 12:20.
- **R1:** Monday's first afternoon gap runs from 12:40, ten minutes after the Bank A call, to 14:50, ten minutes before the Supplier A review: 130 minutes, rounded down to 120 and capped at 120.
- **R3 and R5:** Monday to Wednesday have no morning gap of 60 minutes: Monday 08:30 to 08:50; Tuesday 08:30 to 08:50, and the other gaps are taken by journeys; Wednesday starts with the school run, the time from 10:00 to 10:30 goes on the journeys either side, and the next gap begins at 12:00, after the 30-minute journey back from Client B offices. Thursday's gap runs from 09:40 to 11:40, ten minutes before R4: 120 minutes. Friday's runs from 08:30 to 09:50, ten minutes before the demo: 80 minutes, rounded down to 75. 120 + 75 minutes is 3 h 15 min, 45 minutes short of 4 h.
